# Confidentiality and data-use notice

This repository contains research code developed at the **Indian Institute of Technology
(IIT) Patna** as part of ongoing, unpublished research. It is shared **solely so that
the work described in the author's resume can be validated** ahead of publication. It is not a software release.
Please read this notice before using, sharing or publishing anything derived from it.

## Unpublished research

- The QSSM method is **under review** (target venue: *IEEE Transactions on Medical
  Imaging*). Its core implementation and unpublished analyses are withheld from this
  release (see [WITHHELD.md](WITHHELD.md)).
- Do **not** redistribute, re-publish or present the withheld components, or any results
  obtained with them, without written permission from the principal investigator.
- The author can walk through the withheld components in an interview, without sharing
  the source.

## Data

- **No imaging data is included or redistributed.** This covers raw scans, derived maps,
  ground truth, checkpoints, logs and results.
- **HCP data** must be obtained directly from the Human Connectome Project under its own
  data-use terms (see the main README).
- **Clinical data** from collaborating institutions is covered by separate agreements and
  ethics approvals. It must never be committed, uploaded, attached to issues or shared
  outside the approved research team. Subject identifiers from clinical cohorts must
  never appear in code, comments, logs or issues.

## Lab practice

- Keep the repository **private** until the lab approves a public release.
- Checkpoints, results and figures stay on lab storage, not in git.
- Report any accidental disclosure (data, credentials, identifiers) to the principal
  investigator immediately. Remove it from the full git history, not just the latest
  commit.
